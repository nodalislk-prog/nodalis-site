Chapter IV · The Creator

The hands behind the thread.

Nodalis began with one pair of hands and a single skein of yarn in Colombo — evenings spent learning what thread could become when no one hurried it.

Every piece in this story is still made by those same hands: measured, stitched, unpicked, and stitched again until it is exactly what it was meant to be. Not a factory. Not a batch. One maker, one piece, one person it belongs to.
